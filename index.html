Good to know

A few helpful details.

What comes in each pack, how ordering works, and where to ask about delivery.

What comes in each toothbrush pack?

The bamboo kids’ pack includes two toothbrushes (pink and blue), and the family combo includes two adult and two kids’ brushes. The neem range includes adult and kids’ toothbrushes and a toothbrush with a tongue cleaner. Open a product to see its photos and full details.

How should I care for a natural-handle toothbrush?

Rinse the bristles after brushing, shake off excess water, and store the toothbrush upright in a well-aerated holder. Avoid leaving the wooden handle sitting in water.

How do I place an order?

Add your chosen product to the bag, then check out through WhatsApp. Sign in with your saved delivery address and location so they’re included in the order message. Review the message and send it to ZOHD to confirm your order details.

What are the delivery options and dispatch times?

Shipping is free on orders above ₹499, and orders are dispatched within 48 hours. Cash on delivery is available. Contact ZOHD through the WhatsApp help button to confirm delivery availability and details for your location.
